## What happened in Week 5

- **Turnovers decided it.** Seattle turned it over 4 times, and San Francisco scored 21 points off them. Darnold threw 2 interceptions, both to Renardo Green: one undercutting a third-down throw to JSN on the right sideline, one on a crosser to Elijah Arroyo. Running back fumbles by Emanuel Wilson and George Holani led directly to 49ers touchdowns.
- **Purdy won it late.** He led two fourth-quarter scoring drives, and Eddy Piñeiro's 44-yard field goal after the two-minute warning made it 32–29. Purdy finished 19-of-29 for 189 yards, 2 TDs and 1 INT. Mike Evans scored San Francisco's first TD and caught the two-point conversion that put them up 29–26.
- **Seattle was outgained only 269 to 251.** Darnold went 18-of-29 for 195 yards. One report says San Francisco also turned it over 3 times; I've only seen that in one source.

## Where the teams are now

- Defense: **1st in the NFL in yards allowed** through Week 10 (FootballDB: 236.1 per game). About 17.5 points allowed per game, 4th or 5th depending on the source.

- Las Vegas: 6 sacks (2 from Witherspoon), Raiders held to 27 second-half yards, JSN 8 catches for 171 yards. But 2 lost fumbles and no points in the fourth quarter.
- JSN has 1,207 receiving yards through 10 games.

**San Francisco** (season stats are as of about 9 games, so check them against a current source before using numbers on the show)

- Purdy against Minnesota: 255 yards and 3 TDs.

## Draft: 10 Things to Watch

1. **WHO'S UNDER CENTER, AND DO THEY PROTECT IT**
   *Why:* Darnold threw 2 picks to Renardo Green in Week 5. Lock has thrown none in his two starts.
   *Draft call:* Seattle's quarterback throws **zero interceptions.**

2. **WIN THE TURNOVER BATTLE**
   *Why:* Four giveaways turned into 21 49ers points in Week 5. Seattle lost 2 more fumbles in Vegas.
   *Draft call:* Seattle **wins or ties the turnover margin.**

3. **ATTACK THE NEW RIGHT TACKLE**
   *Why:* McKivitz is out, and Vederian Lowe is in against a front coming off 6 sacks.
   *Draft call:* Seattle gets **at least 3 sacks.**

4. **CAN THE RUN GAME HANG WITH A TOP RUN DEFENSE?**
   *Why:* Seattle has gone 81, then 120, then 150 rushing yards, and San Francisco allows about 82.6 a game. This is the real test of whether the run game has improved.
   *Draft call:* Seattle runs for **100+ yards.**

5. **MAKE McCAFFREY EARN IT**
   *Why:* San Francisco's offense runs through him, and Seattle's run defense has been one of the league's best.
   *Draft call:* McCaffrey finishes **under 100 scrimmage yards.**

6. **JSN VS. RENARDO GREEN, ROUND TWO**
   *Why:* Green jumped the throw to JSN in Week 5. JSN is at 1,207 yards and just had 171 in Vegas.
   *Draft call:* JSN goes over **100 receiving yards.**

7. **KEEP THE NO. 2 OFFENSE UNDER ITS AVERAGE**
   *Why:* San Francisco scores about 31 a game. Seattle allows about 17.5.
   *Draft call:* Seattle holds San Francisco **under 24 points.**

8. **WIN THE FOURTH QUARTER**
   *Why:* Purdy beat Seattle with two late drives in Week 5, and Seattle didn't score in the fourth quarter at Vegas.
   *Draft call:* Seattle **outscores San Francisco in the fourth quarter.**

9. **TOUCHDOWNS, NOT FIELD GOALS**
   *Why:* Seattle went 1-for-2 in the red zone at Vegas. Field goals don't win at Levi's.
   *Draft call:* Touchdowns on **at least half** of Seattle's red-zone trips.

10. **YOUR FINAL CALL**
    *Why:* The final call is yours, not mine.
    *Your call:* Winner and score.
