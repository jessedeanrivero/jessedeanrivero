10 things to watchThe Sunday cheat sheet

Ten quick reads for what matters most once the game starts.

01

Protect the football

Seattle's three turnovers in Washington became 21 points. The Chargers forced five takeaways in Buffalo, so ball security is the cleanest way to keep Los Angeles from hanging around.

02

How the Chargers cover JSN

Jaxon Smith-Njigba leads the NFL with 405 receiving yards and six touchdowns. Watch whether Los Angeles rolls safety help his direction or trusts single coverage.

03

Leonard Williams vs. Justin Herbert

Herbert has been sacked eight times through three games. Williams has a sack in every game, so Seattle's interior pressure has a chance to shape the entire afternoon.

04

Get the run game back

Seattle ran for only 44 yards in Washington. The Chargers have allowed 349 rushing yards through three games, which gives Brian Fleury a good spot to reestablish play action and heavier personnel.

05

Make Herbert solve the disguise

Seattle has allowed only 686 total yards all season. Herbert still has the arm talent to punish a coverage mistake, so the pre-snap and post-snap picture matters all game.

06

Find Ladd McConkey on third down

McConkey leads the Chargers with 12 catches for 183 yards and a touchdown, but he is questionable with a foot injury. Julian Love is cleared to return after practicing fully Friday, which should help Seattle handle motion, spacing and third-down communication.

07

Win in the red zone

The Chargers went one-for-four on red-zone touchdown chances at Buffalo and have scored 14, 14 and 16 points in their first three games. Force field goals and the matchup tilts hard toward Seattle.

08

Respect the Chargers' takeaway ability

Los Angeles lost in Buffalo despite forcing five turnovers. The defense has still created short fields, so Seattle needs to avoid giving them extra possessions.

09

Do not give away hidden yards

Special teams was one of Mike Macdonald's post-Washington focus areas. Field position and kickoff coverage are easy ways to give an 0-3 opponent momentum.

10

Step on them if Seattle gets ahead

If the Seahawks build an early two-score lead, watch whether Macdonald keeps pressure on Herbert rather than backing off. This is the spot to turn a response game into a comfortable win.
