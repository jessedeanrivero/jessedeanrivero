What happenedSeattle took the game away after a 7-7 first half

The Week 2 concern was whether Seattle's offense would leave the defense on the field all afternoon again. The exact opposite happened. Seattle ran 67 plays, held the ball for 36:33, and limited Arizona to 47 offensive snaps.

The score was tied 7-7 at halftime. Seattle then scored 24 unanswered points. Drew Lock finished 19-of-26 for 235 yards, three touchdowns and no interceptions. The offense added 40 rushing attempts, which kept Arizona from getting the possession volume it enjoyed in Week 1.

The headline

The mismatch

The big change
