Enough Free Points.What Week 5 taught us

Seattle fell 32-29 to an unbeaten San Francisco team. The 49ers managed only 269 total yards, yet four Seahawks giveaways supplied 21 points. Darnold threw two picks and was sacked five times. JSN still topped 100 receiving yards, and Jason Myers kicked five field goals.

That's the immediate problem for Mike Macdonald and Brian Fleury. Seattle has championship-caliber stretches of defense, but turnovers and drive-ending mistakes are keeping opponents alive. Two defeats, Washington and San Francisco, have come by a combined five points. A road win in Denver would offer the cleanest possible response.

The War Room question: Will Seattle play with the discipline of a 4-2 team, or keep gifting points to opponents it otherwise controls?

10 Things to WatchHeadlines and scouting triggers for Thursday night

01

NO FREE POSSESSIONS

Four turnovers against San Francisco became 21 opponent points. Protect the snap, pocket and catch point. A clean turnover margin is Seattle's most urgent correction.

02

JSN VS. THE PATCHWORK SECONDARY

Pat Surtain II and Talanoa Hufanga are both on the injury watch. If either sits, Denver's coverage communication matters. Watch who shadows JSN and how often they bracket him.

03

THE BONITTO TEST

Nik Bonitto and Zach Allen helped Denver pressure Justin Herbert in Week 5. Watch Denver's edge width, how Fleury chips, and whether Darnold climbs rather than drifts.

04

FLEURY'S HEAVY PERSONNEL

Watch Brian Fleury's 12, 13 and 21 personnel. Does a fullback or extra tight end force lighter coverage into run fits, then lead to play action? Track the first scripted 15 plays.

05

PAYTON TAKES THE HEADSET

Sean Payton reclaimed play calling from Davis Webb before Denver's Week 5 win. Mike Macdonald now prepares for a different offensive decision-maker on a short week. Look for quick throws and changing tempo.

06

KEEP NIX IN THE POCKET

Bo Nix will try to escape pressure and reset outside. Seattle should collapse the interior with Byron Murphy II and Leonard Williams, then close the edge without losing contain.

07

FIRST DOWN IS THE GAME

Seattle's defense has been stingy with yardage, while Denver has struggled to sustain offense. Force second-and-long and make the Broncos earn every long drive instead of handing over easy field position.

08

TAKE THE SHOT WITHOUT THE RISK

If Denver shades multiple defenders toward JSN, Rashid Shaheed and Cooper Kupp get chances elsewhere. Darnold needs disciplined reads rather than desperation throws into late rotations.

09

WATCH THE BACKFIELD

Denver's J.K. Dobbins is trying to find steady rushing lanes. On Seattle's side, Zach Charbonnet remains an uncertain return. Personnel health could change short-yardage play calling.

10

TOUCHDOWNS, NOT JUST MYERS

Jason Myers converted five field goals in the 49ers loss. Seattle needs red-zone touchdowns and clean execution on the road. Count red-zone entries and points, not only total yards.

Macdonald vs. Payton

Sean Payton took play-calling duties back from Davis Webb after four games. Denver won in Los Angeles in Week 5, but the offense remains uneven. Expect Payton to lean on quicker decisions and favorable matchups. Macdonald's disguises need to survive tempo, shifts and movement.

Fleury vs. Vance Joseph

Denver's pressure fronts have a path to Darnold. Seattle needs to identify pressure, use tight ends and fullbacks intelligently, and protect the middle of the pocket. Denver's banged-up secondary invites an aggressive, disciplined passing plan.
