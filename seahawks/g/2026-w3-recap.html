What happenedSeattle won most of the game except the possession stat that can erase everything else

Seattle outgained Washington by 179 yards, produced 11 more first downs and averaged 2.8 more yards per play. The Seahawks still lost because all three giveaways became Commanders touchdowns.

Jadarian Price's first-quarter fumble created a short field. Sam Darnold's tipped third-quarter interception led to another touchdown. Then Kain Medrano returned Darnold's fourth-quarter interception 50 yards for the score that ultimately separated the teams.

The hopeful read

The most important number on the season is ultimately the one in the win column, and Seattle is 2-1 instead of 3-0. That matters.

But this did not look like a team getting pushed around. Seattle lost by two while outgaining Washington 437-258, winning first downs 28-17 and averaging 6.8 yards per play to Washington's 4.0. The game swung on three giveaways, and Washington turned every one of them into a touchdown.

You cannot guarantee a different result by simply deleting one turnover, because the rest of the game would change too. But in a two-point loss, protecting the ball even once more very plausibly changes the outcome. That makes this a painful loss with a very fixable lesson, not evidence that the first two weeks were fake.
