WHAT DECIDED ITThe win, the progress, and the warning signs.

THE THIRD QUARTER FLIPPED THE GAME

Seattle trailed 10–7 at the half. Jadarian Price scored on a 12-yard run, then Drew Lock escaped pressure and connected with JSN for a 71-yard touchdown. JSN piled up 144 receiving yards in the third quarter alone. Two touchdowns in that period were enough to win.

THE DEFENSE IS STILL THIS TEAM'S FOUNDATION

Six sacks. Five straight three-and-outs during the second half. Only 27 Raiders yards after halftime. Devon Witherspoon had two sacks, while the front kept rookie Fernando Mendoza uncomfortable. Seattle gave up only 163 yards for the entire game.

THE RUN GAME KEEPS IMPROVING

From 81 rushing yards against Chicago to 120 against Arizona to 150 in Las Vegas. Charbonnet ran for 94 on 20 carries, with rookie Price adding 36 yards and his first career touchdown. That's three straight games of improvement.

LOCK WAS EXCELLENT, EXCEPT FOR THE BALL SECURITY

Lock finished 14-of-17 for 240 yards, two touchdowns, zero interceptions and a 158.0 passer rating. He also lost two fumbles, including one on the second offensive snap. When the offense protects the ball, it looks dangerous. The giveaways kept a comfortable game closer than necessary.

JESSE'S WAR ROOM VERDICT

I'll take the win, and I'm happy with the continued progress in the run game and the defense. But we need to eliminate the turnovers and start finishing games with four quarters of execution. The bye week is the chance to clean it up. This defense deserves an offense that doesn't waste possessions.
