The Quick Read

We beat Arizona 31–7 in Week 2 with Drew Lock starting. I think we win again. But dude, at 4–4, I need to see this team execute.

I'm actually more hopeful about the offensive line than I was after Chicago. Grey Zabel is back at left guard, Anthony Bradford is back at right guard, and Josh Jones is a solid backup tackle. I'd love to see us run all over Arizona and give Lock a little room to work.

We've been under-executing for weeks. A win matters, obviously. But I want to see progress that holds up against the next opponent too.

Key MatchupsFour things I'll be watching first.

  • Josh Jones, Zabel and Bradford vs. Arizona's front. Cross and Nelson are out, but Zabel and Bradford get to play their more familiar spots. Josh Jones handled left tackle last week. Josh Sweat is still a concern, but I like this setup better than the constant shuffling.
  • Seattle's run game vs. Arizona. I want to see Fleury lean into the run early. We're asking Lock to start again with an elbow issue. The less this game depends on him playing hero ball, the better.
  • JSN vs. Arizona's secondary. He's sitting at 985 receiving yards. Lock found him for 155 and three touchdowns in Week 2. It's hard not to feel good about that connection.
  • Our defensive front vs. Allgeier and McBride. Allgeier ran for 147 last week, and we're missing Leonard Williams and Brandon Pili. McBride has 66 catches. Macdonald needs answers without his usual front.

What Worries Me

  • We've made too many games harder than they needed to be. Chicago was an ugly reminder. Seattle went 1-for-11 on third down. The talent is here. The execution hasn't been consistent.
  • Red-zone defense. Opponents have scored touchdowns on 83.3% of their trips inside our 20. Chicago went four-for-four. That's hard to ignore.
  • The injuries up front on defense. Williams, Pili and Nwosu are all out. We need somebody else to step up against the run.

What I Like

  • The line might be better than Monday. Zabel back at LG. Bradford back at RG. Josh Jones has been a solid backup tackle. I'm hopeful those familiar spots make the whole thing work better.
  • We've already seen Lock beat Arizona. He threw for 235 yards and three touchdowns in the first meeting. We shouldn't need a miracle from him to win this.
  • I still believe in Macdonald's defense. Honestly, I think this is a generational group. We've got to stop wasting good defensive football with sloppy execution elsewhere.

The Conclusion

Short answer: I think we win. I like the line going back to familiar spots, and I'd love to see us run all over the Cardinals. We've been a capable team playing below our ability for way too much of this season.

I'll take a dub, obviously. But if we're still giving away drives and struggling to execute, I'll be disappointed even with a win. We can't spend this season hovering around .500 because we're underperforming. I believe this is a generational defense. We need clear, sustainable growth on offense so we don't waste it.

That's what I'm looking for on Sunday. I want to see better execution we can repeat next week.
