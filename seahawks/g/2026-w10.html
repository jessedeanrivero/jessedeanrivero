The Quick Read

Seattle beat Arizona 17–3. Our defense held the Cardinals to 198 yards, we showed improvement running the ball, and both red-zone trips ended in touchdowns. I'll take that win, no question. But nine penalties for 83 yards meant we still left too much on the field.

The Raiders are averaging 26.4 points with Kubiak. Their offense is no joke. Their defense, on the other hand, ranks 32nd in total yards allowed (385.4 per game), 32nd against the run (151.2) and 32nd in points allowed (29.8). Seattle's defense leads the league in yards allowed (244.2).

My standard hasn't changed: Win the game. Protect Lock. Build on the run game. Finish drives. Clean up the penalties. I want this to feel like a team improving, not a team surviving.

Key MatchupsWhere this game turns.

MAXX CROSBY VS. THE O-LINE

Charles Cross returned to limited practice Thursday. Quenton Nelson is out. Whether Cross or Josh Jones handles left tackle, I need an actual protection plan for Crosby from the opening drive.

MENDOZA VS. MACDONALD

The No. 1 overall draft pick makes his first start after the Kirk Cousins trade. Seattle's defense should show him multiple looks, change the picture late and force him to process quickly.

SEATTLE VS. THE RAIDERS' RUN DEFENSE

Las Vegas is giving up more than 150 rushing yards a game. We ran for 120 last week. The opportunity to control this game is right here.

BOWERS AND JEANTY IN SPACE

Rookie quarterbacks love an easy completion. Brock Bowers is a major threat, and Ashton Jeanty contributes as a receiver. Win the tackles after the catch and make Las Vegas work for drives.

My Read

Seattle should win this game. Kubiak knows our offense, and his Raiders have weapons, but there's an obvious chance to run the ball against their defense. If our offensive line handles Crosby, Lock shouldn't need to play hero ball.

The defense has been keeping this team relevant. Let's stop wasting that with avoidable penalties or empty trips to the red zone. We're 5–4 with a bye coming. I want momentum, not another reason to ask why our offense hasn't caught up.
