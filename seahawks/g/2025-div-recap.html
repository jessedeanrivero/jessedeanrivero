About the grades. A grade is a percentile. It compares one player's production in this game against every comparable performance in the season: 591 quarterback games, 3,501 skill-player games, 9,753 defensive games. A 90 means the player produced more than 90% of comparable outings. It is built from expected points added per opportunity, completion percentage over expected for quarterbacks, and recorded defensive events.

What a grade is not. It cannot see blocking, coverage, or route running, because public play-by-play does not record them. A receiver who ran a perfect route on a play the quarterback missed gets nothing here. Treat these as production in context, not as scouting. Everything on this page is DERIVED from nflverse play-by-play, FTN charting, and Pro Football Reference snap counts.
