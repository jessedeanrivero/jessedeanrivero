San Francisco entered 4-0 behind an efficient offense. Seattle made Brock Purdy take his first three sacks of the season and limited the 49ers to 14 first downs. Byron Murphy II recorded two sacks, while Ernest Jones IV added another. San Francisco got seven points from a defensive fumble recovery and two short-field touchdown drives after Seattle giveaways.

On offense, the Seahawks scored 29 points despite managing 251 total net yards. Sam Darnold threw two interceptions, while the ground game lost two fumbles. Jaxon Smith-Njigba finished with five catches for 102 yards and a touchdown. Jason Myers was five-for-five on field goals, all from at least 40 yards. Those are excellent individual results. Too many possessions still stalled or ended with the ball going the other way.

Four giveaways. A 21-point bill.

The 49ers scored 21 points off Seattle's four turnovers. Washington did the same with three giveaways in Week 3. Across the two losses, turnovers alone led to 42 opposing points.

Three sacks after four sack-free games

Pressure showed up against an offensive line which had protected Purdy without a sack through four weeks. Seattle pressured and contained a strong offense.

Inches at the end

Cooper Kupp stretched for the first-down marker on fourth down and finished short. San Francisco took over and ran out the clock. A game decided by three points had already swung on larger mistakes.

Last season's 3-2 Seahawks eventually found their identity and finished as champions. The lesson is not that another title is inevitable. It is that an October record does not tell the whole story. In 2025, Seattle recovered from tight defeats and later beat San Francisco 13-3 in Week 18 and 41-6 in the divisional round.

This season's underlying problem is clearer. In Washington, three giveaways led to 21 points. Against San Francisco, four giveaways led to another 21. Two competitive games became two losses despite legitimate defensive performances.

The War Room read: The margin between Seattle and these opponents has been small. The margin for error when giving away the ball has been nonexistent. Clean up the exchanges and decisions before drawing conclusions about how far this team can go.
