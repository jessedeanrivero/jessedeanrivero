The Quick Read

Week 5 was a 32–29 loss at home. Four Seattle giveaways turned into 21 San Francisco points. Darnold threw two interceptions and the run game lost two fumbles. Take away the gifts and that's a different game.

Since then, a lot has changed. Seattle beat Las Vegas 21–13 before the bye. JSN had 171 yards, the defense had six sacks, and the run game climbed for a third straight week. The 49ers beat Minnesota 35–10 in Mexico City, but they lost Purdy to an ankle sprain and right tackle Colton McKivitz to injured reserve.

My standard this week: Protect the ball. Take it away. Run it with the healthy backs. Get after a backup quarterback behind a new right tackle. Finish drives with touchdowns, because field goals don't win at Levi's.

Key MatchupsWhere this game turns.

DARNOLD VS. HIS OWN WEEK 5

Darnold missed the last two games with quadricep and rib injuries from the Week 8 loss to Chicago. He practiced fully Wednesday and Thursday and starts Sunday. Drew Lock showed what simple, clean football looks like. Sam needs to play that game.

OUR PASS RUSH VS. VEDERIAN LOWE

McKivitz is on IR with an ankle injury and is expected to miss the rest of the season. Lowe, who mostly plays left tackle, moves to the right side. That's a matchup we should hammer, Witherspoon corner blitzes included.

OUR RUN GAME VS. THEIR RUN DEFENSE

San Francisco allows about 81 rushing yards a game, among the best marks in the league. Charbonnet and Price are healthy, but Abraham Lucas and Quenton Nelson are out on our line. This is a real test, not a Raiders defense.

McCAFFREY AND MIKE EVANS

Their offense runs through Christian McCaffrey. Mike Evans is questionable with an Achilles injury, and he has a long history of hurting Seattle. Mac Jones will lean on both if he's playing.

10 Things I'm WatchingMy ten calls from the episode, locked before kickoff. We grade them after the final whistle.

01

SAM HAS TO PROTECT THE BALL

Darnold's off the injury report, and Macdonald has been clear that he's the starter. In Sam we trust. But in Week 5, Renardo Green caught two of his passes, and Renardo Green plays for the 49ers. Lock just showed us a simplified game: hand it off, make the sure throws, zero hero ball. Sam doesn't need hero ball to win this. Make the big throw when you need it and take care of everything else.

CALL: Darnold throws zero interceptions.
02

WIN THE TURNOVER BATTLE

Those four giveaways turned into 21 points for the Niners in a 32–29 game. That's unacceptable. We also lost two fumbles in Vegas, including one to open the game. It didn't cost us there, but I want those details buttoned up.

CALL: Seattle wins the turnover battle.
03

GO AFTER THE NEW RIGHT TACKLE

McKivitz is done for the year, and Vederian Lowe slides into right tackle. We had six sacks in Vegas and the defensive line has momentum. You can't ask for anything more beautiful than a Devon Witherspoon corner blitz off the blind side. I hope Mac Jones gets to experience all of it.

CALL: Seattle sacks Mac Jones at least three times.
04

CAN THE RUN GAME HANDLE THIS RUN DEFENSE?

The Niners give up about 81 rushing yards a game. Since Charbonnet and Price got back, we've gone 81, then 120, then 150 on the ground. This isn't the Raiders, and Lucas and Nelson are out, so it's a huge test for the healthy backs.

CALL: Seattle rushes for at least 100 yards.
05

MAKE McCAFFREY EARN IT

Their whole offense runs through Christian McCaffrey. Our run defense has been one of the best in the league for years, and I don't see McCaffrey breaking through on Sunday.

CALL: McCaffrey finishes under 100 total yards, rushing and receiving combined.
06

JSN VS. RENARDO GREEN

Yes, I know they were interceptions. It's still funny to say Darnold completed two passes to Renardo Green. Now Green gets JSN again, and JSN is an absolute menace: 1,207 receiving yards already, including 171 in Vegas.

CALL: JSN beats Green and the 49ers' secondary for 100+ receiving yards.
07

KEEP THE NINERS UNDER THEIR AVERAGE

With Purdy, this has been an electric offense. By my count they're scoring about 31 a game. Purdy isn't playing, and our defense allows 17.5 a game.

CALL: San Francisco is held under its scoring average. My number: 20 points.
08

WIN THE FOURTH QUARTER

Purdy beat us with two late drives in Week 5. Mac Jones is trying to fill those shoes, and I don't think he can.

CALL: Seattle shuts out the 49ers in the fourth quarter.
09

TOUCHDOWNS, NOT FIELD GOALS

We went 1-for-2 in the red zone in Vegas. Field goals do not win at Levi's.

CALL: Seattle scores touchdowns on at least half its red-zone trips.
10

THE FINAL CALL

The Seahawks are better than their 6–4 record. What we saw against the Chiefs is what this team is made of, and this game is where everybody else sees it. Come at me in the comments.

CALL: Seahawks 34, 49ers 20.

My Read

I think the Seahawks win this game. We're better than 6–4, and a win in Santa Clara proves it to everybody. The recipe hasn't changed since Week 5: don't hand them the ball, take it away when Mac Jones gives us chances, run it with Charbonnet and Price, and make that new right tackle miserable.

Lose, and it's 6–5 looking up at a 10–1 Niners team in our own division with a crowded wild card race. Win, and it's 7–4 with two Rams games still coming. Let's go get it.
